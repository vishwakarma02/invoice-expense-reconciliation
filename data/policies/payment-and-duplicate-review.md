# Payment and Duplicate Review

An invoice marked as a possible duplicate or a bank debit that differs from an
invoice total must be reviewed before further payment processing. Compare the
referenced invoice, bank statement, remittance advice, and prior payment ledger.
Determine whether the difference is a partial payment, approved deduction,
duplicate debit, or an unresolved discrepancy; record the supporting evidence
and route unresolved items to a finance reviewer.

Automated matching and anomaly findings are review signals only. They do not
authorize, initiate, or release a payment. Never send a vendor communication
automatically; prepare correspondence as a draft for human review.
