# Invoice Exception Handling

When invoice data does not agree with an approved purchase order, receipt, or
invoice arithmetic, do not treat the automated match as approval to pay. Compare
the cited invoice and purchase-order evidence, confirm the applicable contract
or approved change, and request a corrected invoice or documented approval when
the source documents disagree. Keep the issue open until a finance reviewer
records a decision.

For unit-price differences, compare the invoice unit price with the approved
purchase-order unit price. For line-total and grand-total differences, recalculate
the arithmetic from the cited quantities, prices, taxable value, and tax lines.
Do not substitute an estimated amount for a missing source value.

For tax type, rate, or amount differences, confirm the supplier and buyer tax
jurisdictions and compare invoice tax components with the purchase-order rates.
Refer uncertain tax treatment to the finance or tax reviewer rather than
changing tax values based on an automated explanation.
